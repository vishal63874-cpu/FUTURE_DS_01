# FUTURE_DS_01

## Business Sales Performance Analytics – Future Interns Task 1

### Project Overview
This project analyzes business sales data to identify sales trends, product performance, category performance, regional performance, and profitability.

### Tool Used
- Power BI

### Dashboard Analysis
The Power BI dashboard includes:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Sales by State
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales by Segment
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit

### Key Objective
To analyze sales performance and identify meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making.
